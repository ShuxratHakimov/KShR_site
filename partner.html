World Bank

The World Bank is an international financial institution that provides loans, grants, and expert advice to developing countries. Its mission is to reduce poverty and support sustainable development. The World Bank funds projects in education, health, infrastructure, and climate resilience across the globe.

"We must find new ways to drive inclusive growth and fight poverty in a changing world."

President of World Bank

Ajay Banga

Asian Development Bank

The Asian Development Bank (ADB) is a regional development bank established to promote social and economic development in Asia and the Pacific. It provides loans, grants, and technical assistance to its member countries, supporting projects in infrastructure, education, energy, and poverty reduction.

"Our mission is to achieve a prosperous, inclusive, resilient, and sustainable Asia and the Pacific."

President of ADB

Masatsugu Asakawa

UNDP

United Nations Development Program

The United Nations Development Programme (UNDP) is the UN’s global development network, working in nearly 170 countries to eradicate poverty, reduce inequalities, and promote sustainable development. UNDP supports governments in achieving the Sustainable Development Goals (SDGs) through policy advice, capacity building, and funding.

"Development is not about charity, it’s about opportunity and empowerment."

Administrator of UNDP

Achim Steiner

JICA

Japan International Cooperation Agency

The Japan International Cooperation Agency (JICA) is Japan’s official development assistance agency. It supports economic growth, social development, and disaster resilience in developing countries. JICA provides technical cooperation, grants, and loans to promote peace, stability, and prosperity worldwide.

"Human security and quality growth are the core of our mission."

President of JICA

Akihiko Tanaka
